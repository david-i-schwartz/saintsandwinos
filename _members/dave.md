Dave plays drums and percussion for Saints & Winos, with no click track and no safety net.

By day he teaches game design and development at RIT. By night, he's building the band's live visualizer: an audio-reactive light show, written in openFrameworks, that listens to the band and throws it onto a screen behind the stage (eventually).
